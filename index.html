CashyAI – Account & Data Deletion Request

This page applies to the CashyAI mobile application published on Google Play by BudgetGuru.

CashyAI does not require users to create a traditional username-password account. Google OAuth is used only to allow users to back up and restore app data to their personal Google Drive account.

Important: CashyAI does not store your backup data on its own servers. All backup data resides only in the user’s Google Drive.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

To request deletion of your account association and related data, please send an email from your authorized email address.

Email: ak663904@gmail.com

Email Subject: Delete My Account – CashyAI

What Data Is Deleted

  • Google OAuth access tokens associated with CashyAI
  • Any CashyAI backup data stored in the user’s Google Drive (app-specific data)

Data Retention

After verifying the request, all applicable data will be deleted within 7 business days. No data is retained after deletion.

For any privacy-related questions, you may contact us at the email address above.
